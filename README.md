# -Elecronics-prices
Yearly electronics margin perfomance 

Project Objective

The goal of this project is to conduct a comprehensive analysis of 2023 sales data in order to identify key revenue drivers, evaluate seasonality patterns, and analyze pricing distribution. The insights derived from this analysis are intended to support data-driven decision-making and optimize future sales strategies.

Key Objectives:

 - Assess overall sales performance and monthly dynamics
 - Identify top-performing product categories
 - Analyze price distribution and customer purchasing behavior
 - Detect seasonality trends and revenue fluctuations

Key Insights
-  Overall Stability Amidst Volatility
   Despite noticeable fluctuations in weekly and monthly sales (low linearity), the overall annual trend demonstrates steady growth. This is confirmed by a consistently increasing cumulative revenue curve, indicating          strong   long-term performance.

-  Seasonality Patterns
   Sales exhibit clear seasonal behavior, with observable dips during certain months (notably February and June). These fluctuations highlight opportunities for targeted interventions to stabilize revenue throughout the       year.

-  Top-Performing Products
   A small group of products (top 5) contributes significantly to overall revenue, indicating a concentration of demand. These products play a critical role in driving business performance.

Tools & Technologies:
  Data Analysis: SQL
  Visualization: Power BI
  Data Processing: Excel / CSV
